rlx: privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

rlx is a one-thumb arcade of small relaxing games (Bricks, Catch, Putt and Stack) for Android. This page explains what the app does with your data. In short: nothing.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, record or send any personal or device information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting.

What the app stores

No game data or personal data is stored. The app writes no game state, scores, settings or identifiers to your device, and each game starts fresh every time you open it. Like any Android app, the app's framework and the system WebView keep their own technical files on the device (for example, a cache and the installed version number); these hold nothing about you or your play.

Network

The app's games are inside the app. It does not request internet access and makes no network connections.

Permissions

The app uses one permission: vibration, for the short taps you feel when a ball hits a brick or a block lands. It is granted at install and needs no prompt.

Sharing

Because nothing is collected, nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

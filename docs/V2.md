## 3. Rules that hold (unchanged from v1)

- Android only, portrait, one thumb. Vanilla HTML, CSS, JS, ES modules, no dependencies, no build step.
- Full screen. No text, number, button or HUD anywhere. No points. No sound. Haptics always on. No settings.
- Nothing is stored. Opening a game always starts it fresh.
- Home is the Android back gesture. A touch that begins within 24 CSS px of a side edge is ignored.
- No fail state. **When a game ends it just starts again**: no message, no screen, no tap needed.
- Light and dark follow the phone, live, no toggle.
- Bricks: not one line changes. It is already endless.

## 4. Pool (`games/pool/`)

Beach billiards. The whole screen is the table. No opponent, no rules, no turns: pot any ball in any order.

### Look (the jpg is the reference)
- Ground: `sand`. Cushions: a 4 px `sandWall` band along all four screen edges (inside the safe area), with a 1 px `wetSand` lip.
- Six pockets, drawn as tide pools: sea band 2, a darker middle (sea band 3 in light, `deep` in dark), a 1 px `foam` ring. Four in the corners, two at mid height on the long sides.
- Ten object balls, radius 4 (9 px across), each with the 1 px edge all round, a 2 px highlight and a drop shade. Colours drawn at random per rack from brick rows 0, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and `blue`.
- Cue ball: `foam` in a coral edge.
- A dashed baulk line and its D in `courtLine`, a few sand specks and faint starfish (the decor amounts). Drawing only.
- Dark: the same pieces from the dark token set. No colour literal in the game.
- Contrast: every ball's fill or edge reaches 3:1 on the sand, the cue ball's edge too, the pocket ring too, in both themes. Check it in `tests/contrast.mjs`' way; a colour that fails moves toward `edgeTarget` until it passes, and the changelog says which.

### Table (logical px; width 135, height from the screen)
- Rack: a triangle of 4 rows (1, 2, 3, 4 balls), apex toward the player, apex at 32 percent of the table height, centred.
- Baulk line at 74 percent of the height; D radius 20, opening downward; the cue ball's spot is 9 px below the line, centred.
- Pockets capture, not collide: a ball is potted when its centre comes within the pocket's radius. Corner pockets: radius 9, centred 5 px in from each corner. Side pockets: radius 8, centred on the cushion's inner face. Generous on purpose.

### The shot: little thumb travel
- Touch anywhere, relative: where the thumb lands does not matter. Pull back, the shot goes the opposite way, release to shoot (the gesture Putt had; take `pullShot` and the pull-ring drawing from `archive/v1`, `games/putt/`).
- **Full power at 20 logical px of pull** (15 percent of the screen width, about 53 CSS px on a 360 px phone; Putt's was 48). `pullCapPx: 20`.
- Power curve: `speed = launchSpeed * (pull / pullCapPx) ^ 1.5`, so the short travel still gives soft shots.
- Past the cap the power stays full and the direction keeps following the thumb, so pulling further only steadies the aim.
- A pull of 4 px or less is released as no shot.
- A touch while any ball moves is ignored.
- Aim guide while pulling: a dotted line from the cue ball to its first contact (ball or cushion), a ring where the cue ball will be at contact, and 12 px of the struck ball's line. Always full length, whatever the power.

### Physics (`sim.js`, no DOM, runs in Node)
- Fixed 120 Hz step, sub-steps of at most 1 px, swept so nothing tunnels at top speed.
- `launchSpeed: 300` px/s. Constant deceleration `decel: 38` px/s². A ball stops under 4 px/s.
- Ball on ball: equal mass, restitution 0.96. Cushion: 0.8. No spin.
- Pockets are tested before cushions.
- Never stuck: if anything still moves 12 s after a shot, deceleration triples until all is at rest.

### Play
- Opening: a fresh rack, cue ball on its spot.
- After a shot the cue ball plays from where it stops.
- Cue ball potted: no penalty. After 600 ms it rises on its spot (the nearest free place if a ball is there).
- Last object ball potted: 900 ms later a new rack appears and the cue ball returns to its spot. That is the whole loop.
- A potted ball shrinks into the pool over 240 ms with a small splash ring (none of that with reduced motion: it just goes).
- Pool does not read or advance the session clock and has no dusk change: there is no sky on screen.

### Haptics (the shared `tick`, 40 ms gate)
- Release 10 ms. Ball on ball 6 ms and cushion 4 ms, each only above 20 px/s closing speed. Pot 24 ms. New rack 30 ms.

### Files
- `games/pool/index.html`, `pool.js` (drawing, the thumb, haptics; every play value in one `CONFIG` block at the top), `sim.js`.

### Not included, on purpose
Spin, cue stick, power meter, rules, colours with meaning, an opponent, levels, obstacles, any count.
